# Series Bible — The Green Between (working title)

Keep this updated after every chapter. Short, factual entries only — this is a continuity reference, not prose.

## Premise
A druid-assassin working as a covert loyalist agent for the Elf King infiltrates an orc war-clan to unseat its warlord and break his alliance with the dark lord Azaroth — while a bounty hunter Azaroth has dispatched closes in, drawn by rumors of a newly unearthed amulet.

## Setting / World Facts
- **The Elven Crown**: ruled by King Elyndra, based ~300 miles from the Bloodfang valley. Runs covert operations via the Verdant Blade rather than open war.
- **The Bloodfang Horde**: orc war-clan currently allied with Azaroth in exchange for iron, grain, and protection from "eastern raiders." Camped in a river valley behind a palisade. Warlord not yet named — to be established next chapter.
- **Azaroth's court**: dark lord antagonist; associated with a signature "unmaking" — a cold, old-leather-and-burnt-iron sensation left on things/creatures that have passed through his influence.
- History/backstory established so far: a dragon-bonded warrior from "the northern holds" recently recovered an amulet from a collapsed barrow (rumor only, third/fourth-hand as of Ch.1). Not yet named on the page.

## Magic System
- **Druidic (Verdant Blade) magic**: nature-linked, sensed through touch/contact with water, moss, stone — described as "asking" the natural world questions and getting back impressions/shapes rather than clear facts. Costs attention and calm; used for reconnaissance/sensing more than direct combat so far. Requires physical contact or proximity to the natural medium (water, plant life, stone).
- Source of magic: unspecified yet — implied old/elemental, pre-dating current kingdoms.
- Dragon-bond magic: not yet shown on the page — reserved for the dragon-warrior thread.

## Characters
### Maren Ashwood
- Role/allegiance: Druid warrior, senior agent of the Verdant Blade, loyal to King Elyndra. POV character, Ch.1.
- Physical description: none fixed yet beyond hood/cloak; keep unspecified age (implied experienced, has "buried two" fellow agents) until needed.
- Voice/manner: dry, controlled, teaches through short blunt lines rather than lectures; privately doubts Elyndra without losing loyalty to the mission.
- Goals & secrets: destabilize the Bloodfang warlord's rule from within rather than by assassination; suspects the amulet rumor and the hunter in the woods are connected.
- Relationships: mentor figure to Corvin Rell.

### Corvin Rell
- Role/allegiance: Verdant Blade, two years initiated, younger/less disciplined than Maren.
- Voice/manner: earnest, impatient, still idealistic about the Circle's methods.
- Goals & secrets: wants fast, decisive action; deferential to Maren but pushes back verbally.

### [Bloodfang second-fire orc — unnamed]
- Role/allegiance: unclear; sits apart from the warlord, doesn't look at him, walks off alone along the river at night. Flagged by Maren as a possible rival or defection point.
- To be developed/named in a future chapter.

### [The Hunter — unnamed, unseen]
- Role/allegiance: dispatched by Azaroth. Not yet directly witnessed — only sensed via forest going unnaturally silent and a cold "unmaking" scent (old leather, burnt iron).
- To be introduced properly in a later chapter (possibly the dragon-warrior's thread).

## Artifacts / Institutions
### The Verdant Blade
- What it is: secret druid order of loyalist agents/assassins serving King Elyndra, financed covertly (financier not yet named on the page — user's premise specifies a "shadow underworld" backer, to be introduced).
- Rules/costs: operates by patience and long-game destabilization over direct assassination, per Maren's doctrine — though this may be house style rather than universal Circle law.

### The Amulet (unnamed)
- What it is: recently unearthed from a collapsed barrow in "the northern holds" by a dragon-bonded warrior. Not yet seen on the page — rumor only.
