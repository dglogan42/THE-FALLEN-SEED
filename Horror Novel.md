# Instructions
1. **Pacing:** Build tension slowly using a "sawtooth model" (buildup, peak, and brief relief before building dread again).
2. **Culture & Setting:** Use vivid, localized sensory details rather than relying on generalized western tropes.
3. **Themes:** Intertwine psychological dread with societal issues, grief, or colonial histories.
